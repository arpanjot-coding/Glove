Phone compatibility

Check the operating system and the connection feature you want to use. Model families below help you find the right settings.

On this page Requirements by feature Android phones and tablets iPhone models Windows and network Verify your device

Requirements by feature

Android mirroring and input
Android 11 or newer with Wireless debugging, plus the official Windows scrcpy tools and a shared local network.
Experimental iPhone screen viewing
A compatible Safari screen picker, a verified local certificate, and permission to share the screen. The initial test target is iOS 27 or newer.
Browser file sharing
Android or iPhone with a browser that can open a local HTTP page, select files and download them.
Glove MCP
An authorized Android device visible to ADB and a local stdio MCP client running on the PC.

Android phones and tablets

Look for Wireless debugging in Developer options after updating to Android 11 or newer. The examples below are device families to check. Glove compatibility varies by installed Android version, region, manufacturer firmware, and device policy.

Samsung Galaxy S
Galaxy S21, S22, S23, S24 and S25 families, including applicable Plus, Ultra and FE variants.
Samsung Galaxy A and M
Examples include Galaxy A14, A25, A26, A35, A54, A55, M33 and M54. Check each model’s installed Android version.
Samsung Galaxy Z and Note
Galaxy Z Fold3 and later Fold models, Z Flip3 and later Flip models, and the Galaxy Note20 family on compatible firmware.
Google Pixel
Pixel 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 families, including applicable A, Pro and Fold models.
OnePlus
OnePlus 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13 families, supported Nord models, and OnePlus Open.
Motorola
Recent Moto G, Edge and Razr models, such as Moto G Power (2024), Edge 50 and Razr 50.
Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO
Xiaomi 12 through 15 families, recent Redmi Note models, and POCO F and X series. Check your MIUI or HyperOS Developer options for Wireless debugging.
OPPO, realme and vivo
Recent OPPO Find and Reno, realme GT and Number series, and vivo X and V series on compatible Android builds.
Nothing and CMF
Nothing Phone (1), Phone (2) and Phone (2a), and CMF Phone models with the required Android version and setting.
Other phones and tablets
Check recent Sony Xperia, ASUS Zenfone, Honor, HMD and Nokia phones, plus Android tablets such as Galaxy Tab and Pixel Tablet. Use the same requirements check for a model missing from this list.

Samsung setup. Open Settings → About phone → Software information and tap Build number seven times. Return to Settings to open Developer options. Android’s manufacturer-specific settings guide.

iPhone screen viewing and file sharing

Glove’s experimental viewing flow requires Safari to offer a compatible screen picker. The initial test target is iOS 27 or newer, and physical iPhone playback testing is pending. Follow the certificate verification instructions in Glove before approving screen sharing.

For OS eligibility, Apple lists the iPhone 11 through iPhone 17 families, iPhone Air, iPhone SE (second generation or later), iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and iPhone Duo among iOS 27 models. Check your exact model against Apple’s list. Browser file sharing uses a separate connection and can be tried on earlier iOS versions. Apple’s iOS 27 model list.

Windows and network

Use a Windows x64 PC and connect both devices to a trusted local network that allows them to reach one another. Guest Wi-Fi, managed networks and VPN routing can prevent device discovery or access to the browser link.

Android screen control uses the official scrcpy Windows tools, installed separately. Glove’s browser transfer supports phone uploads up to 2 GiB per file. Available space on the PC and browser behavior can affect large transfers. Official scrcpy installation instructions.

Verify your own phone

  1. Record your model, Android or iOS version, and Glove version.
  2. Connect both devices to your private Wi-Fi and follow the setup for your feature.
  3. Try a small test file in both directions. For Android, check that the mirror shows live frames and responds to mouse input. For iPhone, check that frames update after approving Safari’s picker.
  4. Report the steps and result with test data, omitting pairing codes, session links and personal screen content.
